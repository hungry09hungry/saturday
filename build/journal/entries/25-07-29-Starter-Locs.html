Starer Locs

Finally happy with the way my hair looks

Created: 2025-07-29
Last Edited: 2025-10-15

Putting all the starter locs into my hair took nearly a week this time but it was totally worth it! Now that I'm looking at my hair, I'm kind of scared of what retwists will be like since there are so many (I didn't count but they are pretty small). Until about half way up the back of my head there's an almost even grid parting. But, after that I just started estimating how much hair needed to go into each part. If I hadn't done that this whole thing probably would've taken until the end of the month... I gotta keep my hands out my hair but it's so soft and fluffy.
